Tipping Point Survey

A fire on a grid either dies out or crosses the map, and a small change in the spread control s decides which. Here a simulator and a neural network trained on it burn the same landscape side by side.

Not a safety tool. This is a toy simulation on made-up terrain. It must never inform real fire or evacuation decisions.
